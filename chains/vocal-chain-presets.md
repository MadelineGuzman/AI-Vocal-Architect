# Vocal Chain Presets

Current preset families represented in the system:

- Clean & Natural
- Aggressive Rap
- Chopped & Screwed
- Autotune Vibe
- Smooth R&B
- Gospel / Big Choir Lead
- Lo-Fi / Bedroom Pop
- Drill / Dark Trap

Each preset defines:

- tonal shaping targets
- compression behavior
- de-essing behavior
- ambience direction
- intensity scaling logic

These presets are intended as production starting points, not locked final mixes.
